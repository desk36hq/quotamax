# Quota Advisor

Live Explorer panel for Paseo that tracks your subscription usage, weekly/5-hour reset countdowns, pace multipliers, 15-minute burn-rate ETAs, and **API-equivalent cost estimates** (ported from T3 Code's LiteLLM pricing engine and local Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, and Pi session scanners) — and recommends the best model + thinking effort for `Quick`, `Standard`, `Hard`, and `Frontier` tasks with 1-click Apply.

It also registers the `alibaba` account usage source behind `paseo usage` (`server/alibabaUsage.ts`), which the panel reads for the Alibaba pool.
